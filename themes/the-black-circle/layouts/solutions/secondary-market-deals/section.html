Our focus

We focus on the space industry.

Launch, satellites, in-orbit services and defence-adjacent infrastructure are scaling from niche to strategic. We track the sector continuously and curate a small number of opportunities each year.

Launch and infrastructure — launch vehicles, propulsion and ground infrastructure as the sector's foundation layer.

Satellite connectivity and earth observation — communications constellations and geospatial intelligence with recurring-revenue models.

In-orbit services and logistics — servicing, refuelling, debris management and orbital data centres.

Defence and government demand — sovereign budgets providing durable, contracted revenue for the sector.

How it works
